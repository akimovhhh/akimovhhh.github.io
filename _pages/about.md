I am a PhD candidate in Quantitative Marketing at the Haas School of Business, University of California, Berkeley, and expect to be on the job market in 2028–29. My research sits at the intersection of industrial organization and the economics of information. I study how information arises and spreads in markets, how consumers and firms use it to form beliefs and make decisions, and how emerging technologies such as AI are changing this process. I am advised by [Sarah Moshary](https://sites.google.com/site/sarahmoshary/).
